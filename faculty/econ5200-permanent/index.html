Prerequisite is any introductory econometrics, so it is open to the whole MS cohort rather than one track.

What no other ECON course covers

Double Machine Learning
DiD, IV and RD alongside regularization and cross-fitting. The bridge between 5140 and 5210, taught as extension rather than as a new subject.

“Does this prediction support a causal claim?”
An explicit discipline for the most expensive mistake in applied work. 5210 asks what the data predicts; this asks what it licenses.

Generative AI as an audited workflow
Students integrate AI into analysis documented and verifiable. None of the department’s 38 graduate listings mentions AI — checked against the current catalog.

Replication and critique
Students replicate published empirical work, then deliver a quantified recommendation that states its uncertainty and assumptions.

Labs are diagnostic: students read code they did not write and find what is wrong with it.

What the topics listing costs us

  • It cannot be a prerequisite, so no sequence can be built on it.
  • It cannot appear in program requirements or a concentration — advisors cannot advise toward it.
  • It is invisible in the catalog to prospective MS applicants comparing us with peer programs.
  • Transcripts show a generic title — students applying to jobs and PhD programs have no line describing what they did.
  • Repeat-enrollment limits under one topics number can block students who want more than one topics course.

Every one of these is a cost to students, not to the instructor.
