Section 1

Three Data Structures

10 min block. Section 1, including Poll 2.

Three Ways of Observing the World

Photograph

Many subjects, one instant. 56 countries' Big Mac prices on 1 July 2024.

Movie

One subject, many periods. US GDP, 1947 to today, quarter by quarter.

Surveillance grid

Same 56 countries, every six months, for 25 years.

The structure you have determines the questions you are allowed to ask. A photograph compares but cannot show change. A movie shows change but cannot compare. Only the grid does both.

Draw the 2x2 on the board now — units on one axis, time on the other. Leave it up all session; you point at it during PPP and again during missing data.

The Taxonomy

Notation You Will See All Semester

$y_i$

Cross-sectional — unit $i$, $i = 1,\ldots,n$. Time is frozen.

$y_t$

Time series — period $t$, $t = 1,\ldots,T$. The unit is frozen.

$y_{it}$

Panel — unit $i$ in period $t$. Both move.

The subscripts tell you what varies — and that second dimension is what lets you difference away everything about a country that doesn't change. That is the whole trick behind fixed effects.

Why Platforms Care

400+

concurrent A/B experiments every day at LinkedIn

1000s

monthly tests on Microsoft's Experimentation Platform

Validity depends entirely on the structure: the same units, observed repeatedly, over the same periods.

Without panel structure you cannot separate the effect of the change from the effect of the season, the user, or the day of week. Decisions worth hundreds of millions ride on that distinction.

A Misconception Worth Killing Early

“More data always means better analysis.”

A million rows of cross-section cannot answer a question that requires time variation. Adding countries to a single-period snapshot does not help you measure how exchange rates change.

Structure beats size. A clean panel of 56 countries over 25 years is more powerful than a cross-section of 10,000 countries at one moment — because only the panel supports identification strategies that account for unobserved heterogeneity.

Someone will push back — "big data" is culturally sticky. Ask them what regression they would run on the 10,000-country cross-section to estimate the effect of a policy adopted in 2015. Let them find the wall themselves. The phrase to land it: volume without structure is dead weight — and the trade has a name, identification versus external validity. Choosing the 10,000-country snapshot trades away the ability to identify a cause in order to buy broader coverage.

Section 2

Purchasing Power Parity

10 min block. Section 2. The board work is the slow part; the four lines are not.

A Burger in Two Currencies

United States

$5.79

Switzerland

CHF 7.20

If goods markets worked perfectly — no tariffs, no transport, no local wage differences — what should the exchange rate be?

The one that makes the burgers cost the same.

The Economist Big Mac Index, January 2025.

Do This on the Board First

Implied PPP  =  7.20 ÷ 5.79  =  1.24 CHF per USD

Actual spot  =  0.90 CHF per USD

Valuation  =  (1.24 − 0.90) ÷ 0.90  =  +38.0%

The franc is overvalued by 38 percent. A Swiss burger buys more dollars than the exchange rate says it should.

Numbers before abstraction. Compute this by hand in front of them — do not show the formula until after. Students should watch the arithmetic happen.

Now Generalise

$$\text{Implied PPP} = \frac{P_{\text{domestic}}}{P_{\text{US}}}$$

$$\text{Valuation \%} = \frac{\text{Implied PPP} - \text{Spot}}{\text{Spot}} \times 100$$

Say the sign convention out loud. Positive means the local currency is overvalued: the burger is expensive relative to what the exchange rate implies. Negative means undervalued.

The Whole Index at a Glance

Four Lines

latest = df[df["date"] == "2025-01-01"].copy()
us = latest.loc[latest["iso_a3"] == "USA", "dollar_price"].iloc[0]

latest["implied_ppp"]   = latest["local_price"] / us
latest["valuation_pct"] = (latest["implied_ppp"] - latest["dollar_ex"]) \
                          / latest["dollar_ex"] * 100

The economics was the hard part; the pandas is bookkeeping. That ratio should be your baseline expectation for this course.

Before You Trade On It

“The Big Mac Index proves currencies are misvalued.”

It illustrates PPP logic; it does not price currencies. A Big Mac is mostly non-tradeable inputs — rent, wages, utilities, tax.

Swiss burgers are expensive because Switzerland is expensive, not because the franc is 38% too strong. It is a teaching instrument, not a trading signal.

Pakko & Pollard (2003), "Burgernomics: A Big Mac Guide to PPP," FRB St. Louis.

Section 3

Missing Data Is Structure

8 min block. Section 3, including Poll 3.

Filter for Russia

Observations from 2000 through early 2022 — and then nothing.

Russia did not stop having a currency. McDonald's sold its 850 Russian restaurants after the February 2022 invasion; the buyer rebranded them Vkusno i Tochka — “Tasty and That's It.”

That same shock moved exchange rates, triggered sanctions, and changed every outcome the index measures.

The reason the data is missing is the reason the data would have been interesting.

An Unbalanced Panel, Made Visible

Rubin (1976): Three Mechanisms

MCAR

Unrelated to anything, observed or not. A server crashed. Benign — you lose power, not unbiasedness.

MAR

Depends on observed variables. Younger respondents skip the salary question, but within an age group it is unrelated to salary. Correctable.

MNAR

Depends on the unobserved value itself. High earners refuse because they are high earners. Dangerous — no amount of observed data fully fixes it.

Two Questions, Three Verdicts

Poll 3: Diagnose the Mechanism

Russia's rows in the Big Mac Index stop in early 2022, after the invasion and the sale of McDonald's Russian restaurants.

Which mechanism is this?

A) MCAR — a collection accident
B) MAR — the war is observable
C) MNAR — depends on the unobserved values
D) Not missing — Russia left the sample
Students click to vote on their devices. Results render live on the next slide.

Answer: C — MNAR

MAR requires that conditional on what you observe, missingness is unrelated to the missing value. Russia's post-2022 burger prices and exchange rates were driven by sanctions — the same force that removed them. Conditioning on “there was a war” does not recover them.

The trap: B — the seductive trap of controlling for an observable event. About half the room will pick it. Yes, the war is observable — but the mechanism runs through the unobserved values. Worth 30 seconds. And give D a nod: if your estimand is “countries where McDonald's operates,” Russia genuinely left the population. Change the question, change the diagnosis.

The Same Mechanism, at National Scale

UK Labour Force Survey response rate

97.5% → 83.6%

in three years

People whose rates it sets

67 million

Bank of England mandate

Who stops answering a government survey? People with two jobs and no time. High earners who value privacy.

If non-respondents differ systematically on employment and wages — the very things being measured — that is MNAR. The Bank may have held rates too high, too long, because of a missing-data mechanism.

Two Results Worth Carrying Forward

Heckman (1979). Sample selection on unobservables is mathematically equivalent to omitted variable bias. We observe wages only for people who work; if working correlates with potential wages, the observed average is biased upward. The two-step correction models the selection explicitly and adds the inverse Mills ratio as a regressor.

Survivorship bias. Anthropic's AI Economic Index (March 2026) found high-tenure users were disproportionately successful at complex tasks — but new users who got no value abandoned the tool and vanished from the panel. Same mechanism as mutual fund returns and clinical trial dropout.

Missing data is not noise to be cleaned away. It is a structural feature to be modelled.

Heckman (1979), Econometrica 47(1). Full treatment in the chapter reading.

Key Takeaways

Data is not given — it is built. Every dataset encodes measurement decisions that constrain what you can conclude.

Structure determines method. Only panel supports the identification strategies you came here for.

PPP is a theory you can compute. Four lines of pandas. The limitations come from the measurement, not the code.

Missing data is information. MCAR/MAR/MNAR is a diagnosis, not a cleanup step. Russia's absence is a signal.

Reproducibility is professional. Brodeur et al. (2024): 85% of papers ran, 25% carried coding errors in data manipulation, only 70% survived robustness checks. random_state=42, every time.
